Privacy, in plain words

Last updated: 7 November 2026

Who runs this site

This is the personal site of Excel Adila Putra. Questions or deletion requests can go to Exceladila123@gmail.com.

What is stored

  • Contact messages — the name, optional email, optional subject, message text you submit, and a one-way hash of the network address used to send it. The raw IP address is not stored. Messages are visible only to the owner and stay until they are deleted.
  • Site ratings — the star score and a one-way network hash, so one network cannot vote repeatedly. No name or email is attached.
  • Feedback — the message you type, shown as “Anonymous”. It can be sent more than once.
  • Rubik leaderboard — the display name you choose, a random device ID, your best time, and the scramble used for it. Everyone can see this board.
  • Gallery — photo captions published by the owner.

What stays on your device

The site uses browser localStorage and sessionStorage, not advertising cookies, for things like:

  • your theme choice;
  • your Rubik display name, random player ID, and local solve history;
  • whether you have already rated the site;
  • a short-lived secret-page access token, which expires automatically.

Clearing site data in your browser removes these.

The private photo page

The hidden page is protected by a password checked on the server. Its photos are not public files and are only returned after a correct password creates a temporary access token. The password itself is never stored in the page or in the repository.

Services used

The site is hosted by GitHub Pages. Data such as messages, ratings, feedback, and leaderboard entries is stored in Supabase. Fonts, scripts, and images are served from this site rather than third-party CDNs. There is no third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking pixel.

Your choices

  • You can send a message without giving an email address.
  • You can use the site without rating it or joining the leaderboard.
  • You can ask the owner to delete a message, rating, feedback entry, or leaderboard entry by writing to the email above. Include enough detail to identify the entry.
  • If you are under 13, please ask a parent or guardian before sending a message.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top will change too. The short version: the site collects what it needs to run the guestbook, ratings, and leaderboard, and nothing else.
